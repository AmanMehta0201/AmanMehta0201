### About

I'm a full stack developer who likes building things properly, not just the UI, but the database design and architecture too. I like taking a project from idea to something people actually use, and I'd rather ship something and fix it based on real feedback than overthink it upfront.

Right now I'm working on **Kaiseki**, a study platform to help people prepare for the JLPT. Before that I built **ChessInit**, a chess coaching platform where students can book sessions, pay online, and get everything synced to their calendar automatically.
